# Cutframe — A player that belongs to the page.

Cutframe is a Video.js v10 skin that adapts its colors and typography to the surrounding page, helping developers integrate video without designing a separate player palette.

Its cut-corner frame carries through into the time lens. Press and drag the timeline to unfold real video frames, chapter context, and a moving fine ruler around a fixed cursor. Release, and the lens folds back into the timeline.

The demo shows the same player adapting to three distinct page designs while playback continues—keeping its own character as it becomes part of each page.
