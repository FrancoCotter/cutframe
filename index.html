How this player adapts

Cutframe reads page styles and automatically looks for a readable background canvas. Five patches around and behind the player supply color groups; dominant colors shape the surface, secondary colors shape accents, and text is checked for contrast. Single-color scenes stay tonal. Persistent changes update the theme; brief flashes are held. Manual host colors take priority. Unreadable or unsupported sources use a stable fallback.
